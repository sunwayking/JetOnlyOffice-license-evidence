Modification notice

ru_RU_oo3.aff and ru_RU_oo3.dic are historical encoding-transformed versions
of ru_RU.aff and ru_RU.dic from Apache OpenOffice Russian Dictionary 0.6.
ONLYOFFICE stored the original KOI8-R bytes as Windows-1251 characters encoded
as UTF-8 with a byte-order mark. The _oo3 filenames distinguish these modified
versions from the current ru_RU payloads.

JetOnlyOffice mirrors the locked ONLYOFFICE Git blobs without further payload
modification. TRANSFORM.md and verify-transform.ps1 document and verify the
historical transformation and the relevant dates and source versions.
